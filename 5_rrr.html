STEP 3: CREATE

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Three habits that cut waste. Start with the easy tips, then make something new from things you would throw away.

1

Reduce

Use less so there is less to throw away.

  • Bring a tumbler and a reusable bag
  • Say no to single-use items
2

Reuse

Give things a second life before they become trash.

  • Turn jars into storage
  • Repair or pass on old clothes
3

Recycle

Turn clean materials into new products.

  • Rinse and dry before sorting
  • Check your local bin rules
